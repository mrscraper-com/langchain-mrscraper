---
title: "MrScraper service migration"
---

The integration now exposes seven services. Use `mrscraper_account`, `mrscraper_scrape_html`, `mrscraper_scrape_markdown`, `mrscraper_get_screenshot`, `mrscraper_scrape_brand`, `mrscraper_scrape_json`, or `mrscraper_web_search`. See the [SDK README](https://github.com/mrscraper-com/langchain-mrscraper#readme) for schemas, migration, and examples.
